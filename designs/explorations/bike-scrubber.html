Twelve workshop toys that take the bike apart.

Round 2. Your four picks from round 1 come first, then eight new ones in the same spirit: real workshop things, drawn in the bike's own style, with physics you want to play with. Drag one, flick it, or tab to it and use the arrow keys. The bike follows whichever one you last touched.

Leave them alone for a while and, every so often, one gives a small tug, as if to say "try me". Only one control moves at a time, never one you've just used, never while you're using the page, and never if you've asked for reduced motion.

Every one runs on one shared spring engine with fixed time steps, at 60 fps or better, on transforms and plain SVG or canvas. Animation sleeps when nothing moves and when a control is off screen.
