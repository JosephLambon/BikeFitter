Together Apart

What you’re looking at.

A modern road bike with disc brakes, drawn from the drive side: the side with the chain. Its proportions come from a real mid-size endurance frame.

  • Seven systems

    Frame, fork, cockpit, contact points, drivetrain, brakes and wheels, plus a bottle cage.

  • Three contact points

    You touch the bike at the saddle, the handlebar and the pedals. These are where fit is set.

  • UK names

    Parts use UK terms, such as chainset and rear mech. The glossary will list the US names too.
