Format

What’s in every lesson.

One idea per lesson, kept short. Every lesson follows the same pattern, so you always know where to look.

  1. What you’ll learn

    One sentence, up front.

  2. The idea

    Plain English, with a diagram where it helps.

  3. Key takeaway

    The one thing to remember.

  4. Try it

    Something safe to check on your own bike.

  5. Sources

    Who said it, with an evidence label.
